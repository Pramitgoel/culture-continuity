Culture Continuity
Helping you stay connected to your roots and explore new cultures, wherever you go.
Culture Continuity is your companion for finding cultural communities, events, and resources in your new city or campus, while also helping you understand and engage with local cultures.
How to Use:
  1. Enter your details: Tell us your destination, languages, religion, and cultural interests.
  2. Preserve your culture: Discover communities, events, and spaces to stay connected with your heritage.
  3. Explore local cultures: Learn about the cultural landscape, compare values, and find integration guides for your new home.
